Strategy · Fund Ⅲ

Concentrated. Conviction-led. Operator-supported.

A small portfolio. Real positions. Long horizons. The mechanics that protect founders — and the operating depth that compounds the investment after the wire clears.

Thesis

Most venture firms are in the index business. We're in the conviction business. We make 8 to 12 investments per year, lead the round, take a board seat, and stay at the table for the next decade. That's not patience — that's the only way concentrated portfolios actually work.

Our edge is in three places: where we look (four sectors with deep operator networks), how we underwrite (research-driven, with a dedicated team tracking 14,000+ companies), and what we do after (operating partners, recruiting depth, finance and legal infrastructure on call).

We're not the firm that writes 80 checks a year and prays for power-law math. We're the firm that writes 10 checks and stays in the work.

· Investment criteria What we underwrite · What we pass on

What gets our check.

Pre-seed through Series B. $500K to $8M initial check. Lead or co-lead. We take board seats. We stay for ten years.

ⅠStage · 01

Pre-seed → Series B

Initial checks $500K–$8M. Reserves of 2–3× initial for follow-on through Series C. Lead or co-lead, never passive.

ⅡSector · 02

Four sectors · No fifth

AI, Fintech, SaaS, Biotech & Frontier. We pass on every other vertical, no matter how good the deck.

ⅢTeam · 03

Operators with a wedge

Founders who've spent time inside the problem. A defensible wedge, even if the market is still small. Conviction, not consensus.

ⅣTerms · 04

Founder-friendly mechanics

Single-class voting. 1× non-participating preference. No predatory anti-dilution. We win when you win — not before.

· What we will not do The negative space matters more than the positive

Discipline is the strategy. So is the no.

The decisions that protected our LPs in 2022 were not the deals we did. They were the deals we passed on — the bridge rounds at impossible valuations, the AI wrappers without moats, the consumer plays without distribution.

  • We do not invest outside our four sectors
  • We do not pay up for hype cycles or brand premium
  • We do not take passive positions or follow rounds we couldn't have led
  • We do not write checks without a clear board path and reserve plan
  • We do not accept terms that punish founders to inflate IRR
