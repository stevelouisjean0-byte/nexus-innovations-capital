About · NYC, since 2018

Built by operators. Run by them too.

A small venture firm in Lower Manhattan, deploying capital across three funds into the founders building AI, fintech, SaaS, and frontier technology — from first check to public market.

The firm

Nexus Innovations Capital was founded in 2018 by three operators who had each built and exited companies in the sectors we now fund. We started with a thesis that hasn't changed: the best returns come from concentrated portfolios, long horizons, and the kind of operational depth most firms outsource.

Three funds in, we manage $420M across 87 active portfolio companies. We've led 31 rounds. We've held positions through 14 unicorn outcomes. And we've stayed small enough — eleven full-time investment professionals — to actually do the work.

We invest in pre-seed through Series B, with initial checks of $500K to $8M. We lead. We take board seats. We run cap-table mechanics that protect founders. And we stay at the table for seven to ten years.

· Operating principles How we work · What we will not do

A short list. We hold ourselves to it.

ⅠPrinciple · 01

Concentrated portfolios.

8 to 12 investments a year. Each one gets a partner sponsor and our full attention. Coverage is for indexes.

ⅡPrinciple · 02

Founder-friendly by default.

Single-class voting. Reasonable preference. No board games. Terms designed for compounding, not extraction.

ⅢPrinciple · 03

Long horizons.

7 to 10 years per holding. We do not optimize for fund recycle dynamics. The companies that compound do not do so quarterly.

ⅣPrinciple · 04

Operators in every chair.

Every partner has founded, scaled, or operated. We've been in the room. We know what the work is.
