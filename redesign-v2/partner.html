Partner With Us

Let's build what comes next — together.

Three clear pathways into a partnership with Nexus. Choose the one that fits, tell us about yourself, and a partner will respond within two business days.

Pathway 01

For Investors

Who it's for: Individuals, family offices, and institutions seeking disciplined exposure to emerging technology across public and private markets.

What Nexus provides: Access to a vetted private-market pipeline, research-led public-market analysis, transparent reporting, and direct partner relationships.

How to begin

1.Submit your name, contact details, and the type of exposure you're seeking through our contact form.
2.A partner reviews your inquiry and responds within two business days.
3.We schedule an introductory conversation to understand your objectives and share how we work.
Start an Investor Conversation
Pathway 02

For Founders

Who it's for: Founders building in AI, SaaS, fintech, biotech, Web3, or frontier technology — from early stage through growth.

What Nexus provides: Strategic funding, engineering-led diligence that respects your work, and hands-on operating support in strategy, talent, and go-to-market.

How to pitch Nexus

1.Send a short description of your company, your stage, and what you're raising — a deck link helps but isn't required.
2.Our team reviews every founder inquiry; a partner responds within two business days.
3.If there's a fit, we move to a working session on your product and market — not a pitch performance.
Pitch Nexus
Pathway 03

For Strategic Partners

Who it's for: Firms, institutions, and networks interested in co-investment, deal flow, market intelligence, or innovation partnership.

What Nexus provides: Co-investment opportunities, structured partnership support, and access to our network across technology and capital markets.

How to explore a partnership

1.Tell us about your organization and the kind of partnership you have in mind.
2.A partner responds within two business days to explore mutual fit.
3.We scope a specific, structured collaboration — starting small and compounding.
Explore Partnership

Not sure which pathway fits?

Send us a note — we'll route it to the right partner and respond within two business days.
