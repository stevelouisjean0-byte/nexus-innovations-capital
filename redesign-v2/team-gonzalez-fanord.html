General Partner

Gonzalez Fanord

Gonzalez Fanord is a strategic business leader with over 20 years of experience driving enterprise transformation and leading cross-functional teams across the U.S. and globally. He brings deep expertise in telecommunications, IT infrastructure, and large-scale program delivery, helping organizations navigate complexity and execute with precision.

He currently serves as an executive at Nexus Innovations Capital, where he leads operational diligence and portfolio optimization across investments in artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and emerging deep tech. Gonzalez plays a central role in evaluating transformative technologies, scaling founder operations, and aligning strategy with sustainable growth.

As President of JLM & S Enterprises, Gonzalez advises on mergers, acquisitions, and post-integration execution, overseeing strategy, capital planning, and operational alignment. His prior leadership roles include Senior Enterprise Service Delivery Manager at Dataprise Inc., where he drove multi-site optimization initiatives, and a decade-long tenure at CenturyLink/Savvis, where he scaled service delivery teams, managed multi-million-dollar portfolios, and transformed technical operations across key markets.

A lifelong technologist, Gonzalez is a passionate advocate for innovation, actively exploring the impact of artificial intelligence and quantum computing on enterprise systems, workforce transformation, and future infrastructure. He remains deeply engaged in emerging technologies, bringing a forward-thinking lens to every initiative.

Gonzalez is recognized for building high-performing teams, establishing operational discipline, and translating strategy into action across diverse stakeholders. Whether guiding merger integrations, leading network modernization efforts, or managing globally distributed teams, he brings structure, rigor, and a people-first approach to every engagement.

He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, Education, and Technology from the City University of New York, holds certifications in ITIL and ITSM, and is PMP-trained.
