Investment Focus

Where we deploy capital and conviction.

Nine areas where technology, capital, and strategy converge — each selected for durable growth and long-term structural change, and each held to the same evaluation discipline.

01

Artificial Intelligence

Why it matters
AI is restructuring how every industry creates and captures value — from software and finance to science itself.
What we look for
Applied AI companies building durable advantage through proprietary data, model quality, and real-world deployment — not demos.
How we evaluate
Technical diligence on architecture, data rights, and unit economics of inference; commercial diligence on retention and workflow depth.
How we support
Go-to-market strategy, technical hiring, and positioning across the public–private AI landscape.
02

SaaS

Why it matters
Recurring-revenue software remains the most reliable engine of compounding enterprise value in technology.
What we look for
Vertical and AI-native software with strong retention, expanding accounts, and a clear path to category leadership.
How we evaluate
Net revenue retention, payback periods, competitive moats, and how deeply the product is embedded in customer workflow.
How we support
Pricing and packaging strategy, sales motion design, and operational scaling support.
03

Fintech

Why it matters
Financial infrastructure is being rebuilt in software — expanding access, efficiency, and the reach of modern markets.
What we look for
Payments, capital-markets infrastructure, and embedded finance with genuine regulatory readiness.
How we evaluate
Compliance posture, unit economics through cycles, counterparty risk, and defensibility of the underlying rails.
How we support
Capital-markets expertise, structuring guidance, and introductions across our financial network.
04

Biotech

Why it matters
Computation is compressing the cost and time of scientific discovery, creating a new generation of life-science companies.
What we look for
Science-driven teams translating breakthrough research into meaningful health outcomes, with computational biology as an accelerant.
How we evaluate
Scientific validation, milestone structure, capital efficiency, and the strength of the founding research team.
How we support
Long-horizon capital planning and strategic partnership development.
05

Web3

Why it matters
Decentralized infrastructure, where it solves real coordination problems, represents a durable architectural shift.
What we look for
Selective positions in infrastructure with real utility, real users, and disciplined fundamentals — not token speculation.
How we evaluate
Actual usage versus incentivized activity, protocol economics, security posture, and regulatory exposure.
How we support
Institutional discipline in a sector that often lacks it: governance, treasury strategy, and risk management.
06

Public Markets

Why it matters
Public technology leaders set the benchmarks — and the signals — for where private innovation is heading.
What we look for
Innovation leaders and structural-growth companies whose fundamentals justify long-term positions.
How we evaluate
Research-led analysis of fundamentals, competitive position, and durability of growth — rigor over momentum.
How we support
Our public-market work feeds the same research engine that sharpens our private-market conviction.
07

Private Markets

Why it matters
The most significant value creation in technology now happens before companies ever reach public markets.
What we look for
Early and growth-stage opportunities sourced through our network, in sectors where we hold genuine expertise.
How we evaluate
Founder quality, market timing, technical substance, and alignment of structure with long-term outcomes.
How we support
Flexible capital and operating partnership from first check through follow-on rounds.
08

Emerging Technology

Why it matters
Frontier areas — including the AI–quantum intersection — will define the next decade of structural change.
What we look for
Teams working at genuine technical frontiers where early conviction, carefully sized, compounds into meaningful positions.
How we evaluate
Deep technical review by our engineering-led diligence practice; disciplined sizing that respects frontier uncertainty.
How we support
Patient capital, research partnership, and connection to the broader Nexus network.
09

Strategic Founder Support

Why it matters
Capital is abundant; committed operating partnership is scarce. Founder outcomes improve when both arrive together.
What we look for
Exceptional founders at every stage who want a partner in strategy, operations, and access — not just a line on the cap table.
How we evaluate
Fit between what the founder needs and what we can genuinely deliver — we commit where our support moves outcomes.
How we support
Hands-on work across strategy, talent, go-to-market, and capital planning, at every stage of growth.
