Biography

Gregory Pascal is a Managing Partner of Nexus Innovations Capital, an emerging venture capital firm focused on backing bold founders building high-impact, scalable solutions in underserved and high-growth markets. With a career spanning over 20 years across finance, infrastructure, and public affairs, Gregory brings a deep, cross-sector understanding to early-stage investing.

Before launching Nexus Innovations Capital, Gregory spent over a decade in the U.S. financial industry, specializing in capital markets and structured finance. He later transitioned into the public sector, managing complex infrastructure and development projects and advising on strategic planning and policy implementation.

Gregory holds a master’s degree in Public Affairs from Sciences Po Paris and a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from Pace University in New York. His work is driven by a belief that “smart capital, when aligned with purpose, can accelerate meaningful and sustainable progress.” At Nexus Innovations Capital, he is focused on building a platform that not only invests in innovation but helps shape it.
