Biography

Gregory Pascal serves as General Partner at Nexus Innovations Capital, an emerging venture capital firm focused on backing bold founders building high-impact, scalable solutions in underserved and high-growth markets.

His professional journey spans over 20 years across finance, infrastructure, and public affairs. He spent more than a decade in the U.S. financial sector specializing in capital markets and structured finance before transitioning to the public sector, where he managed infrastructure projects and advised on strategic planning.

Gregory believes smart capital, when aligned with purpose, can accelerate meaningful and sustainable progress. At Nexus Innovations Capital, he focuses on building a platform that not only invests in innovation but helps shape it.

He holds a Master's degree in Public Affairs from Sciences Po Paris and a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration from Pace University in New York.
