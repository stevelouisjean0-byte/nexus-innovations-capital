Biography

Gedeon Dieudonne Jr. is a seasoned professional with over two decades of leadership experience in public service and organizational management. His background spans law enforcement and real estate investment.

He brings 20+ years in law enforcement, where he managed high-stakes operations, including large-scale personnel deployments for major events and oversight of multimillion-dollar departmental budgets. His professional background in real estate contributes additional expertise in financial modeling, asset management, and investment strategy.

As a General Partner at Nexus Innovations Capital, he focuses on startup investment and advisory across sectors including securities, AI, logistics, and financial services. His work emphasizes operational discipline, financial insight, and the team-under-pressure rigor of running large-scale operations.

Areas of expertise include venture capital and startup advisory, financial services and securities, strategic operations and logistics, budget oversight and personnel management, real estate investment, and business accounting.
