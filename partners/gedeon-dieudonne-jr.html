Biography

Gedeon is a seasoned professional with over two decades of leadership experience in public service and organizational management. As a member of Nexus Innovations Capital, a Queens-based venture capital firm, Gedeon brings a unique blend of operational discipline, financial insight, and strategic planning to the startup investment space.

His 20+ years in law enforcement honed his ability to manage high-stakes operations, including large-scale personnel deployments for major events, day-to-day command responsibilities, and oversight of multimillion-dollar departmental budgets. He has a proven track record in managing complex systems, ensuring organizational efficiency, and leading teams under pressure—skills that directly translate to identifying, investing in, and advising high-potential startups.

Gedeon also has professional experience in real estate, adding depth to his understanding of financial modeling, asset management, and investment strategy.

At Nexus Innovations Capital, we focus on helping startups grow in sectors such as securities, artificial intelligence, logistics, and financial services. Our founders mission is to support entrepreneurs, especially in the 19–50 age range, by offering advisory, planning, portfolio management, and accounting services that turn visionary ideas into sustainable ventures.
