Biography

Gonzalez Fanord is a strategic business leader with over 20 years of experience driving enterprise transformation and leading cross-functional teams across the U.S. and globally. His career spans telecommunications, IT infrastructure, and program delivery.

At Nexus Innovations Capital, Gonzalez leads operational diligence and portfolio optimization in artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and emerging deep tech. He is also President of JLM & S Enterprises, an M&A advisory and post-integration execution firm.

Prior to Nexus, he served as Senior Enterprise Service Delivery Manager at Dataprise Inc., and held a decade-long tenure at CenturyLink/Savvis managing service delivery, portfolios, and operations.

His core competencies include execution, strategic planning, portfolio delivery leadership, global IT team leadership, and business technology management. He is described as a passionate advocate for innovation, exploring AI and quantum computing's impact on enterprise systems and workforce transformation.

Gonzalez holds a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, Education, and Technology from City University of New York, and ITIL and ITSM certifications. He is also PMP-trained.
