Biography

A seasoned Business Technology Professional with over a decade of experience in the automotive industry and brings a unique blend of technical expertise, operational leadership, and hands-on industry knowledge. Skilled in leveraging technology to streamline processes, improve safety, and optimize performance across complex business environments. He holds degrees in Applied Sciences and has shared his expertise as an Adjunct Professor for over a decade at his alma mater, mentoring the next generation of automotive professionals, while staying connected to academia and industry best practices.

Throughout his career, he has successfully led cross-functional teams, implemented innovative solutions, and driven efficiency in both operations and technology integration. With a deep understanding of fleet management, safety compliance, and process improvement, Smith is passionate about bridging the gap between technology and practical field operations.

Recognized for his adaptability, problem-solving skills, and commitment to continuous improvement, Smith thrives in roles that require both strategic vision and operational execution. His extensive background in automotive systems, sanitation operations, and business technology uniquely positions him to deliver impactful solutions that align with organizational goals and community. He believes wholeheartedly in giving back and serves as a mentor for Automotive Y.E.S. (Youth Educational Systems).

Smith currently serves as an executive at Nexus Innovations Capital, the ventures capital arm of Nexus Innovations Capital.
