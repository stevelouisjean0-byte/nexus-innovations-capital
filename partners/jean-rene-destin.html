Biography

Jean René Destin brings a deep operational lens to Nexus Innovations Capital, where he leads technical due diligence across AI, cybersecurity, telecommunications, and quantum computing investments. With over two decades of experience building and scaling complex network architectures, Jean René helps early-stage companies turn breakthrough technologies into resilient, scalable infrastructure.

Prior to joining Nexus, he was a senior executive in the telecommunications industry, leading cross-functional teams in design, traffic engineering, and service deployment. He is also the founder of Link2Four, LLC, a telecom consulting services company that delivers infrastructure and talent solutions for next-generation networks.

Known for his hands-on approach, Jean René bridges the gap between vision and execution, diving deep into systems, stress-testing assumptions, and guiding founders in building strong technical foundations. He is also passionate about inclusive innovation, mentoring underrepresented founders, and advising in emerging markets.

Jean René holds a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering from The City College of New York and a Master of Science in Telecommunications and Information Management from NYU Tandon School of Engineering.
