Focus Areas · Four sectors

Where we have unfair access.

We don't invest broadly. Each sector below has a partner anchor, a research lead, and a deep operator network. The work happens before the deck arrives.

Ⅰ · Artificial IntelligenceAnchored by · Daniel Reyes

The compute economy enters its second act.

Foundation models commoditize. Inference economics dominate. Vertical AI starts replacing horizontal SaaS. We invest across the stack — infrastructure, agents, vertical applications — with conviction in the picks-and-shovels and the products both.

  • → Foundation model infrastructure (training, inference, serving)
  • → Agent platforms and orchestration
  • → Vertical AI for legal, healthcare, finance, and operations
  • → Compute economy: chips, data centers, cooling, power
Ⅱ · FintechAnchored by · Lena Park

The financial primitives that come next.

Embedded finance, capital markets infrastructure, payments rails, and the back-office systems banks have neglected for two decades. We focus where regulatory complexity creates moat — and where operator depth means everything.

  • → Capital markets infrastructure and B2B payments
  • → Embedded finance for vertical software
  • → Wealth management infrastructure (advisor tools, RIAs)
  • → Regulatory technology and risk infrastructure
Ⅲ · SaaSAnchored by · Daniel Reyes

Vertical software for under-built industries.

The next decade of SaaS belongs to AI-native applications and vertical software for industries that horizontal tools have failed. We back operator-founders building for industries they grew up inside.

  • → Vertical SaaS for trades, healthcare, legal, real estate
  • → AI-native applications replacing horizontal incumbents
  • → Developer infrastructure and platform tools
  • → Open-source-led commercial businesses
Ⅳ · Biotech & FrontierAnchored by · Marcus Chen

Where deep tech hits its commercial year.

Computational biology and ML-driven drug discovery. Robotics, energy, climate. The deep-tech verticals at the moment a research demo becomes a defensible business. Long horizons, technical depth, and patience capital.

  • → Computational biology and TechBio
  • → Industrial robotics and physical AI
  • → Energy infrastructure (storage, fission, fusion-adjacent)
  • → Longevity therapeutics and aging research
