04 · Investment philosophy Long horizon · Concentrated · Operator-led

We invest like the next decade is the only one that matters.

We make 8–12 investments per year. Each one gets a partner sponsor, a board seat, and a 7-to-10-year horizon. That's not patience — that's pattern recognition. The companies that compound do not do so on a quarterly cadence.

We back contrarian conviction. We don't follow the round; we lead it. We don't chase the brand; we underwrite the team and the wedge. And we stay in the work alongside the founder — through pivots, restructurings, and inevitable down rounds — because that's where the real returns live.

  • Concentrated portfolios — conviction over coverage
  • Long horizons — 7 to 10 years, not 3
  • Operator partnerships — we sit with founders, not above them
  • Founder-friendly terms by default — built for compounding
